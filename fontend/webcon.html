FREQUENT INQUIRIES

Everything You Need to Know

What is the advance booking timeframe?
For larger events, please contact us as early as you can. For smaller orders, we will always try to help when our schedule allows.
Do you host private menu tasting sessions?
Ask us about tasting options when you contact us. We can discuss the best way to help you feel confident about your menu.
How do you handle dietary requirements?
Please include dietary requirements and allergies in your request. We will clearly explain the options available for your order.
What staff and tableware are included?
Every order is different. We will outline what is included, from food and packing to delivery or serving help, before you confirm.
