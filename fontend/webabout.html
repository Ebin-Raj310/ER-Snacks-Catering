Where We Started

From our kitchen to your table

ER Snacks began with a simple idea: make tasty food that feels homemade and is easy to share. Our team puts time and care into every batch, from the first preparation to the final packing.

Today, we prepare snacks, meals, and catering orders for birthdays, family get-togethers, office lunches, and all the small moments that bring people together.

We believe good service is personal: listen carefully, cook honestly, and make sure every guest leaves happy.
